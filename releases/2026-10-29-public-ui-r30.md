## Important warning-reception limit

An individual JMA bulletin or advisory may be retrieved and presented without proving that **every** relevant bulletin has been received, that a warning has been rescinded, or that the presented observation is still the most current.

- “Unverified” and delayed / unavailable feeds must not be interpreted as “no warning,” “cleared,” or “safe.”
- Current official announcements and evacuation instructions from JMA and municipalities take precedence.
- Public production does **not** use the QA-only R22 `status.php` evidence endpoint as an authority for an all-clear.
- Offline maps and predownloaded geographic data do not provide new warnings when the network is unavailable.

## Deliberately excluded from production

- R25 / R25A / R25B registered-region feature: **HOLD** because of severe performance issues. Not activated.
- Unapproved volcanic hazard GIS and automatic alert authority: **NOT enabled**.
- QA test/synthetic alert features must not be exposed or interpreted as current official warnings.
